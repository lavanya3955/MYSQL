===============================================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW
===============================================================================

This project is based on a real-world online grocery inventory dataset and
demonstrates the complete workflow of a SQL Data Analyst. It covers
understanding the dataset, importing data into MySQL, performing exploratory
data analysis (EDA), cleaning the data, answering business questions using SQL,
and generating meaningful business insights and recommendations.

===============================================================================
BUSINESS SCENARIO
===============================================================================

QuickMart is a fast-growing online grocery delivery company that offers a wide
range of products, including fruits, vegetables, dairy products, beverages,
snacks, personal care items, and household essentials.

Customers can browse products, place orders, and have their groceries delivered
to their doorstep through the QuickMart mobile application.

Every day, thousands of products are listed, updated, and managed on the
platform. To efficiently store and manage this large volume of product
information, QuickMart relies on a relational database.

The management wants to analyze product pricing, discounts, inventory levels,
and product availability using SQL. The insights generated from this analysis
help the business make informed decisions related to pricing strategies,
inventory management, and product availability.

3. UNDERSTAND THE DATASET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before writing SQL queries, we should understand the dataset.

• A dataset is simply a collection of related data stored in rows and columns.
 This dataset contains more than 3000 grocery products sold by QuickMart.

• Each row represents one product.
Each row represents a unique SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) for a product. Duplicate product names exist
because the same product may appear multiple times in different package sizes, weights, discounts, or 
categories to improve visibility – exactly how real catalog data looks.

Each column stores specific information about that product.

Columns:

1. Category
   Indicates the type of product.
   Examples: Fruits & Vegetables, Dairy, Snacks, Beverages,
   Packaged Food, Personal Care, Home Care, Baby Care.

2. Product Name
   Name of the product.
   Examples: Fresh Banana, Amul Gold Milk, Maggi Noodles,
   Coca-Cola Soft Drink, Lay's Chips.

3. MRP (Maximum Retail Price)
   The original price of the product before any discount.
   Example: ₹120.

4. Discount Percentage
   Percentage of discount offered on the product.
   Example: 10%, 20%, 35%.

5. Discounted Selling Price
   Final price paid by the customer after applying the discount.
   Example: MRP ₹120 with 20% discount → Selling Price ₹96.

6. Available Quantity
   Number of units currently available in inventory.
   Example: 150 units.

7. Weight in Grams
   Weight of the product.
   Examples: 250g, 500g, 1kg.

8. Out Of Stock
   Indicates whether the product is currently unavailable.
   TRUE = Out of Stock
   FALSE = Available for Purchase

9. Quantity
   Number of items or units in a single pack.
   Examples: 1, 2, 6, 12.

10.sku_id: Unique identifier for each product entry (Synthetic Primary Key)

6. DATA EXPLORATION (EDA)
===============================================================================

Understand the data before starting the analysis.

Examples:

• Total Products
• Total Categories
• Product Categories
• Highest Price
• Lowest Price
• Average Price
• Inventory Availability
• Product Availability

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 1. Total Number of Products

SELECT COUNT(*) AS Total_Products FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 2. Total Number of Categories

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT category) AS Total_Categories FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 3. Display All Categories

SELECT DISTINCT category FROM QuickMart ORDER BY category;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 4. Highest MRP (Original Price)

SELECT MAX(mrp) AS Highest_MRP FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 5. Lowest MRP (Original Price)

SELECT MIN(mrp) AS Lowest_MRP FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 6. Average MRP

SELECT ROUND(AVG(mrp),2) AS Average_MRP FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 7. Highest Selling Price

SELECT MAX(discountedSellingPrice) AS Highest_Selling_Price FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 8. Lowest Selling Price

SELECT MIN(discountedSellingPrice) AS Lowest_Selling_Price FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 9. Average Selling Price

SELECT ROUND(AVG(discountedSellingPrice),2) AS Average_Selling_Price FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 10. Total Inventory Available

SELECT SUM(availableQuantity) AS Total_Inventory FROM QuickMart;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 11. Products In Stock vs Out Of Stock

SELECT outOfStock, COUNT(*) AS Total_Products
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY outOfStock;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 12. Product Names Appearing Multiple Times

SELECT name, COUNT(*) AS Number_of_SKUs
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY name
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
ORDER BY COUNT(*) DESC;
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
-- 13. Total Number of Out-of-Stock Products

SELECT COUNT(*) AS Out_Of_Stock_Products
FROM QuickMart
WHERE outOfStock = 1;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 14. Total Number of Available Products

SELECT COUNT(*) AS Available_Products
FROM QuickMart
WHERE outOfStock = 0;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-- 15. Total Inventory by Category

SELECT category, SUM(availableQuantity) AS Total_Inventory
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY Total_Inventory DESC;

===============================================================================
CHECKING FOR NULL (MISSING) VALUES
===============================================================================

Purpose:

Before analyzing the data, we need to ensure that all important fields
contain valid values.

This query checks whether any product has missing information.

If any column contains NULL, that record will be displayed.

If no rows are returned, it means there are no missing values in the dataset.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Query:

SELECT *
FROM zepto
WHERE name IS NULL
   OR category IS NULL
   OR mrp IS NULL
   OR discountPercent IS NULL
   OR discountedSellingPrice IS NULL
   OR weightInGms IS NULL
   OR availableQuantity IS NULL
   OR outOfStock IS NULL
   OR quantity IS NULL;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Missing values can lead to:

• Incorrect calculations
• Wrong reports
• Inaccurate business insights
• Errors in data analysis

Therefore, checking for NULL values is an important step in Data Cleaning.

Example:

Suppose a product has:

Product Name : Amul Milk
MRP          : NULL
Discount     : 20%

Can we calculate the selling price correctly?
No.

So, we first identify and handle missing values before performing analysis.

How to Fix?

Option 1: Update the missing values.

Example:

UPDATE QuickMart
SET availableQuantity = 0
WHERE availableQuantity IS NULL;

UPDATE QuickMart
SET discountPercent = 0
WHERE discountPercent IS NULL;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Option 2: Delete incomplete records (only if necessary).

DELETE FROM QuickMart
WHERE mrp IS NULL;

2. CHECKING FOR DUPLICATE RECORDS
===============================================================================

Purpose:

Find products that appear more than once.

Query:

SELECT name, COUNT(*) AS Total_Records
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY name
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Note:

This does NOT always mean duplicate data.

Example:

Amul Milk 500ml

Amul Milk 1L

Amul Milk 2L

Same product name,
but different pack sizes.

These are different SKUs.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

How to Fix?

If they are genuine duplicate rows,
remove the extra records.

(Use only after verification.)

Example:

DELETE q1
FROM QuickMart q1
JOIN QuickMart q2
ON q1.name = q2.name
AND q1.category = q2.category
AND q1.mrp = q2.mrp
AND q1.quantity = q2.quantity
WHERE q1.sku_id > q2.sku_id;

Keep one record and remove the duplicates.

8. SQL ANALYSIS
===============================================================================

1. Which are the top 10 most expensive products based on MRP?

SELECT name, category, mrp
FROM QuickMart
ORDER BY mrp DESC
LIMIT 10;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Which are the top 10 cheapest products based on MRP?

SELECT name, category, mrp
FROM QuickMart
ORDER BY mrp ASC
LIMIT 10;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Which products have the highest discount percentage?

SELECT name, category, discountPercent
FROM QuickMart
ORDER BY discountPercent DESC
LIMIT 10;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Which products are currently out of stock?

SELECT name, category
FROM QuickMart
WHERE outOfStock = TRUE;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. Which category contains the highest number of products?

SELECT category, COUNT(*) AS Total_Products
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY Total_Products DESC;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Which category offers the highest average discount?

SELECT category,
ROUND(AVG(discountPercent),2) AS Average_Discount
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY Average_Discount DESC;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Which category has the highest total inventory available?

SELECT category,
SUM(availableQuantity) AS Total_Inventory
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY Total_Inventory DESC;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. Which products have no discount?

SELECT name, category, mrp, discountPercent
FROM QuickMart
WHERE discountPercent = 0;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. Which products have the maximum difference between MRP and Selling Price?

SELECT name,
category,
mrp,
discountedSellingPrice,
(mrp - discountedSellingPrice) AS Discount_Amount
FROM QuickMart
ORDER BY Discount_Amount DESC
LIMIT 10;

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. Which product names appear multiple times (different pack sizes/variants)?

SELECT name,
COUNT(*) AS Number_of_Variants
FROM QuickMart
GROUP BY name
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
ORDER BY Number_of_Variants DESC;

9. BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Interpret the results.

Based on the SQL analysis, the following business insights can be derived:

1. Product Pricing

• Premium-priced products were identified using the MRP column.
• Budget-friendly products were also identified for price comparison.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Discount Analysis

• Some products offer significantly higher discounts than others.
• Certain categories provide better average discounts, making them attractive
  to customers.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Inventory Analysis

• Some products are currently out of stock.
• Categories with higher inventory indicate better stock availability.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Product Distribution

• Some categories contain a larger number of products compared to others,
  indicating a wider variety for customers.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. Product Variants

• Several product names appear multiple times because they are available
  in different pack sizes, weights, or quantities.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Pricing Strategy

• The difference between MRP and Selling Price helps identify products
  receiving the highest discounts.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Category Performance

• Average MRP and average discount vary across categories, indicating
  different pricing and promotional strategies.

10. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
===============================================================================

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve
business performance.

1. Improve Inventory Management

• Frequently out-of-stock products should be restocked quickly to avoid
  losing potential sales.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Optimize Discount Strategy

• Review products with extremely high discounts to ensure profitability.
• Offer discounts strategically instead of applying them uniformly.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Focus on High-Demand Categories

• Maintain sufficient inventory for categories containing a large number
  of products.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Improve Product Pricing

• Compare MRP and Selling Price regularly to maintain competitive pricing.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. Monitor Product Availability

• Track products that repeatedly become out of stock and improve inventory
  planning.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Promote Low-Discount Products

• Products with little or no discount can be promoted through special
  offers or marketing campaigns.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Analyze Product Variants

• Similar products available in multiple pack sizes should be priced
  appropriately to encourage customer purchases.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. Regular Data Quality Checks

• Periodically validate the dataset for missing values, duplicate records,
  and invalid data to ensure accurate reporting.
